# School Maintenance Log Book App
## Project Overview

The School Maintenance Log Book App is a Line-of-Business (LOB) solution designed to modernise the way schools report and track maintenance issues across school facilities.

The application replaces traditional paper-based maintenance log books commonly used by staff to report damaged furniture, faulty equipment, building defects and other maintenance concerns throughout the school premises.

## Problem Statement
Many schools rely on physical maintenance log books located in staff rooms or administration offices for reporting issues.

This approach presents several challenges:
- Reports can be overlooked or delayed.
- Handwritten entries may be difficult to read.
- Staff have limited visibility of reported issues.
- Tracking the status of repairs is difficult.
- Historical records are cumbersome to search and analyse.
- Multiple issues may be duplicated due to lack of visibility.

As a result, maintenance requests can take longer to address, impacting staff and student experiences.

## Solution
The School Maintenance Log Book App provides a centralised digital platform for reporting and tracking maintenance issues.

Staff can submit maintenance requests from any device by providing:
- Location
- Issue Category
- Description
- Priority

Requests are automatically recorded, assigned a status and made available for review by relevant staff.

## Objectives
- Eliminate paper-based maintenance reporting.
- Improve visibility of maintenance requests.
- Reduce response times.
- Maintain a searchable maintenance history.
- Provide a standardised reporting process.
- Improve communication between staff and maintenance teams.
